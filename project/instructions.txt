HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Use the main menu to prepare, then start the fight:

  1. Start game       - begin the fight against the aliens
  2. Show aliens      - list the aliens
  3. Quit             - save your progress and leave
  4. Move to another room (Village, Forest, Cave)
  5. Collect the item in your current room
  6. Show your inventory
  7. Look around the current room

IN A FIGHT
  1. Attack - hurt the alien (a defeated alien gives you 10 reward points)
  2. Hide   - 50% chance the alien will not find you
  3. Run    - you lose 5 reward points

You win by defeating all the aliens. You lose if your health reaches 0.
